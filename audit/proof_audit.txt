PROOF AUDIT - SUBMISSION MANUSCRIPT
This is an analytic review and provenance record, not a formal proof-assistant verification.
All formal statements in the submission are listed below. Computational checks are supplementary.
No assertion is made that every theorem in the untouched research archive has been revalidated.

1. Eremenko's fixed-conjugate-degree question [prob:linear]
Check: Finite zero set is explicit. Bidegree convention is (analytic, conjugate).
Literature/simplification: Eremenko 2026, p. 1; the rectangular quadratic bound is n^2+m^2, not n^2.
Source: core:problem

2. Rectangular upper bound and optimal leading coefficient [thm:linear]
Check: Regularize without loss; choose a smooth connected divisor; count positive quotient double points; compute degree m-n; smooth and apply Thom. Radial examples prove the limit.
Literature/simplification: Kronheimer-Mrowka Theorem 1 is restated as Lemma thom; the intersection computation is classical.
Source: b:thm:main

3. Uniform lower bound and the elementary quadratic range [thm:rectlower]
Check: Check s=max(0,3m+1-n), k-s>2(m-s), exact radii, angular multiplicity k+s, local replacement s^2, and bounded global remainder. The statement only claims the range proved in this paper.
Literature/simplification: The radial proof replaces long local-model constructions for the range m<n<=2m+1. Wider archived ranges are outside the submission's claims.
Source: new:thm:rectangle;d:thm:main (restricted range)

4. Exact shifted Chebyshev count [thm:cheb]
Check: Use unique elliptic coordinates, one transverse crossing iff theta<phi, gcd equality count, two small levels, finite-end and infinity-end asymptotics, and nonzero planar Jacobian. Resonant sin(m theta)=0 is explicitly treated by the largest nonzero Chebyshev coefficient.
Literature/simplification: Elementary Chebyshev identities; the conclusion supplies deterministic order mn examples discussed by Lundberg.
Source: h:thm:Cheb

5. Explicit degree-one conjugate example [cor:explicit]
Check: epsilon=1/n^2 is strictly below the smallest threshold using sin(pi/n)>=2/n, sinh(1/n)>=1/n, cosh(1)<4. Multiplication by -i gives p_n-bar z.
Literature/simplification: The count 3n-2 and its prior sharpness are credited to Khavinson-Swiatek and Geyer. The explicit coefficient and direct proof are supplied here.
Source: New explicit specialization of h:thm:Cheb

6. Restricted deterministic construction problem [prob:deterministic]
Check: The family epsilon z^n+q+bar q is distinguished from all harmonic polynomials.
Literature/simplification: Lundberg 2023, Section 4.2 (author version pp. 9-10).
Source: b:thm:harmonic (motivation)

7. Exact maximum in the restricted harmonic family [thm:monomial]
Check: Upper bound includes vanishing low coefficients, singular roots, and a shared origin. Lower bound uses one rational phase and one coefficient scale for all lines; no independent choice of line coefficients is assumed.
Literature/simplification: Refines Lundberg's Descartes argument; sign-independent real-rootedness is credited before the dominance lemma.
Source: b:thm:harmonic

8. Sete-Zur coefficient conjecture [conj:sparse]
Check: Original Remark 4.4 concerns all polyanalytic polynomials of total degree d and d^2 distinct zeros, not only harmonic polynomials.
Literature/simplification: Sete-Zur 2024, Remark 4.4, author version pp. 18-19; journal article 128595.
Source: q:thm:quartic (motivation)

9. Sparse quadratic extremizers and the minimal quartic [thm:sparse]
Check: Odd and even degree constructions are counted before the optional constant shift. Quartic radii are given exactly. A finite binomial has <=2d zeros, proving minimality in degree four.
Literature/simplification: The binomial argument is elementary polar decomposition. No stronger optimality claim is made for d>=5.
Source: q:thm:quartic;q:thm:even;q:cor:supportall;q:prop:binomial

10. All parameters for the m=1 Chebyshev family [prop:allparameters]
Check: The logarithmic derivative of sinh(t)/cosh(nt) strictly decreases. This proves exactly two, one, or zero roots per selected branch. The sole critical point is nondegenerate, giving the stated singular parameters.
Literature/simplification: Direct calculus avoids the gcd and translated-branch argument entirely.
Source: New strengthening

11. Crossings of translated Chebyshev branches [lem:crossing]
Check: X_theta decreases in theta; X_theta-X_phi strictly increases with height for theta<phi, starts below a>2, and tends to infinity. Strict derivative proves transversality.
Literature/simplification: Elementary calculus, retained because a precise unique-crossing statement is needed.
Source: h:lem:crossings

12. Counterexamples to the proposed Wilmshurst count [cor:wilm]
Check: Difference is (m-1)(n-m-1)+1-gcd(m,n); handle n-m=2 separately, leaving only (2,4).
Literature/simplification: Wilmshurst Remark 2 and the earlier disproof of Lee-Lerario-Lundberg are credited.
Source: h:cor:Wilmshurst

13. Parametric transversality [lem:transversality]
Check: Finite dimensional second-countable manifolds, smooth map transverse to the chosen submanifold, residual parameter conclusion.
Literature/simplification: Standard input: Hirsch, Differential Topology, Chapter 3, Section 2. Restated before use.
Source: Classical input

14. Opposite perturbations at a rank-one zero [lem:rankone]
Check: Analytic implicit coordinates yield (x,g); isolatedness gives c y^ell. Rescaling y=t^(1/ell) gives C^1 convergence to c u^ell +/- beta. Uniformity is in a small finite-dimensional family.
Literature/simplification: Uses only the analytic implicit function theorem and simple-root persistence. A constant perturbation alone would not preserve the total count.
Source: b:lem:rankone

15. Regularization without increasing either degree [lem:regularization]
Check: Parameter evaluation is onto at all zeros outside the finite rank-zero set. Choose one direction avoiding finitely many real and complex subspaces; both signs are generic. One sign preserves count; vanishing order strictly decreases at each surviving rank-zero point.
Literature/simplification: Parametric transversality replaces ad hoc common-factor decomposition and extends to derivative-stable coefficient spaces.
Source: b:lem:regularization;core:regularizationspace

16. Smooth connected transverse generic divisor [lem:generic]
Check: The full linear system separates first jets and two-point values. At fixed points an invariant complex tangent would have a fixed real tangent. Connectedness follows because disjoint effective components would all lie in one ruling.
Literature/simplification: Classical jet transversality and the elementary intersection pairing on CP1 x CP1.
Source: b:lem:generic

17. Genus and intersection formulas [lem:adjunction]
Check: For O(n,m), adjunction with K=(-2,-2) gives genus (n-1)(m-1). The two ruling classes have square zero and mutual intersection one.
Literature/simplification: Hartshorne Chapter V Proposition 1.5 supplies adjunction. The intersection pairing is its standard CP1 x CP1 specialization.
Source: Classical input

18. Quadratic bound with a pure analytic leading term [lem:pure]
Check: The graded leading monomials z^d,w^d are coprime, so the quotient has length d^2; equality of distinct real-slice roots forces all local lengths to be one and the real Jacobian to be nonzero.
Literature/simplification: Buchberger's coprime-leading-monomial criterion; equivalently projective Bezout with no intersection at infinity. The short algebraic proof avoids extra hypotheses.
Source: d:lem:pure

19. Thom genus theorem [lem:thom]
Check: Smooth, closed, connected, oriented embedded surface of positive degree d in CP2. Orientation of the constructed surface is reversed before invocation.
Literature/simplification: Kronheimer-Mrowka 1994, Theorem 1. This is the only deep topological input.
Source: Classical input

20. Quotient immersion and signs [lem:immersion]
Check: At the fixed sphere, kernel(dPhi)=J(TL), and the complex tangent is transverse to both TL and J(TL). Nonfixed intersections form two-point orbits. The orientation reversal on the second sheet cancels the ambient reversal, so every double point is positive.
Literature/simplification: Explicit symmetric-product local coordinates, with genus/intersection formulas restated earlier.
Source: b:lem:immersion

21. Real-root count from coefficient dominance [lem:dominance]
Check: Increasing positive sample radii have coefficient signs; IVT supplies the lower count and Descartes bounds multiplicity. Apply again to -r. Roots at zero are excluded by the wording.
Literature/simplification: Related to Passare-Rojas-Shapiro and Forsgard-Novikov-Shapiro; the proof is an elementary sparse-exponent version.
Source: b:lem:dominance

22. Exact radial maximum and rational attainment [lem:radial]
Check: The squared-radius polynomial has two disjoint exponent blocks. A positive common root would produce an infinite circle and is excluded. The explicit rational coefficients attain all sign changes; separate root intervals prove coprimality; radial and angular derivatives prove regularity.
Literature/simplification: Descartes and the preceding dominance lemma suffice; numerical root finding is not used in the proof.
Source: new:thm:radial

23. Elementary local polynomials with d^2 nonzero zeros [lem:models]
Check: Odd degrees use a=0,b=(d-1)/2. Even degrees use bar z times a radial block with angular degree d+1, plus one regular origin. A small rational constant moves the origin; the pure-leading bound excludes extras.
Literature/simplification: Simplifies the local models needed for this paper to the radial lemma; no folding-polynomial or phase analysis is needed for the stated range.
Source: d:prop:seeds (alternative proof)

24. Replace an isolated conjugate monomial by s^2 zeros [lem:split]
Check: Compact-scale C^1 convergence gives the desired zeros. A quantitative annulus estimate excludes intermediate scales. Compactness away from old zeros and a unique highest monomial exclude distant new roots.
Literature/simplification: Real implicit function theorem plus the explicit models; existence, rather than every small perturbation, is asserted.
Source: new:lem:center

25. Transfer m^2 zeros from infinity [prop:transfer]
Check: All added exponents n-i,m-j are nonnegative. Inversion gives initial term v^m; after scale t the limit is the conjugate of Q_m. These zeros escape and are disjoint from the n^2 old zeros. A generic rational perturbation and the rectangular upper bound give exactness.
Literature/simplification: Elementary inversion and persistence; the generic step is stated explicitly to ensure the finite-set bound applies.
Source: d:prop:transfer

26. Descartes bound with one exponent gap [lem:gap]
Check: Internal gaps contribute at most their lengths to the total positive/negative variation. Treat actual lower degree d<M separately, including a zero leading low coefficient.
Literature/simplification: Refinement of the Descartes argument used by Lundberg; complete proof retained because the even-gap sign is essential.
Source: b:lem:harmonicgap
